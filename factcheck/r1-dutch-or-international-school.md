## Missing for expat parents
- Funded international departments (DIS) may make the parental contribution compulsory (Kamerstuk 22452 nr 88, 19 Dec 2023). The cost section mentions only the voluntary one at Dutch state schools.
- Bilingual primary (tpo, 30 to 50 percent of teaching time in English, German or French, per Nuffic) is not mentioned.
- Rijksoverheid words tto as "the other language"; Nuffic describes English for secondary. The page says English, which matches Nuffic.
- Tax: employer-paid international school fees (see r1-thirty-percent-ruling).
