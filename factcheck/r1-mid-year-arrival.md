# r1 mid-year-arrival

RO = https://www.rijksoverheid.nl

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Children can register at any time of the year | partly correct | VO: if you move you can register all year ("het hele jaar aanmelden"); otherwise VO has a regular window from mid-March. The primary page says nothing on time of year. Suggest: "If you move house, you can register at secondary school all year. For primary school, ask the school." |
| Secondary registration works any time if you move | correct | RO/themas/onderwijs/voortgezet-onderwijs/vraag-en-antwoord/voortgezet-onderwijs/inschrijven-voortgezet-onderwijs |
| Register at more than one school | correct (primary) | "U mag uw kind bij meerdere scholen aanmelden" (RO vraag-en-antwoord/basisonderwijs/wanneer-kind-aanmelden-basisschool); schools may have no places or a waiting list |
| Bring passport, school reports, proof of previous school | partly correct | sources name an identity document or birth certificate and proof of deregistration. School reports are not named. |
| No BSN: school can use temporary education number | correct | RO .../burgerservicenummer-bsn/bsn-in-het-onderwijs |
| Extra support: register at least 10 weeks before the start date, in writing | partly correct | Source: at least 10 weeks before the new school year starts, in writing, say at registration that support is needed (RO documenten/vraag-en-antwoord/passend-onderwijs/hoe-krijgt-mijn-kind-passend-onderwijs). It does not address mid-year arrival, so "start date" stretches it. Suggest: "The rule is to register at least 10 weeks before the new school year. If you arrive mid-year, register as early as you can and say at once that your child needs support." |
| School has 6 weeks to decide, one extension of up to 4 weeks | correct | same page. Missing: if no decision after 10 weeks and the child is at no school, the child has a right to a temporary place. |
| Newcomer class can take time | not verifiable | general statement |
