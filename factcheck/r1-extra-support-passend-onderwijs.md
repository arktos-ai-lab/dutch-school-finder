# r1: guide extra-support-passend-onderwijs (checked 2026-11-25)

Source of the law text: repository.officiele-overheidspublicaties.nl XML of WPO (BWBR0003420), WEC (BWBR0003549), WVO 2020 (BWBR0044212), current version of 2026-08-01 (wetten.overheid.nl itself blocked fetching). Article numbers below are from that text.

## Claims

1. "the school where you register ... must offer a suitable place, in the school itself or at another school" (lede, duty of care). Verdict: partly correct. WPO art 40 lid 4-5: a school may refuse a pupil who needs extra support only after it has, in consultation with the parents, arranged that another school is willing to admit the child. Lid 5/6 (WPO) say this does NOT apply (a) if the school is full, or (b) if the school asks parents to respect its religious or philosophical basis and they refuse. Rijksoverheid: "De school waar u uw kind aanmeldt moet uw kind een passende onderwijsplek geven." https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/passend-onderwijs/vraag-en-antwoord/hoe-krijgt-mijn-kind-passend-onderwijs
   Suggested: "...the school must offer a suitable place, at the school itself or at another school. This does not apply if the school is full, or if it has a religious basis and you will not respect it."
2. "A school cannot simply say no and leave you to look." Verdict: partly correct, same exceptions as 1 (school full, denominational basis). Also art 40 lid 4: it only applies to pupils who need extra support.
3. "If it cannot, it must give its reasons and offer a place at another school". Verdict: partly correct. The statute says the school must first arrange that another school is willing to admit the child, after consulting you; a duty to give reasons is not in art 40 itself (WVO 8.8 lid 4 requires a reasoned written refusal only for bijzondere scholen). Suggested: "...it must, after talking with you, make sure another school is willing to take your child before it can refuse."
4. "covers children with learning, behaviour or health needs, and also children who need more challenge". Verdict: correct as description; but see missing item A (Dutch language support is excluded by law).
5. Step 1 "register in writing at least 10 weeks before the start of the school year." Verdict: partly correct. WPO art 40 lid 2 / WVO 8.8 lid 1: "De ouders doen de aanmelding zo mogelijk ten minste 10 weken voor de datum waarop toelating wordt gevraagd." It is "if possible", counted from the date admission is asked for (not necessarily the start of the school year). Rijksoverheid states it without the caveat. Suggested: "register in writing, if possible at least 10 weeks before the date you want your child to start."
   For the move from group 8 to secondary school this is replaced by the central week 25-31 March (WVO art 8.8 lid 8), not 10 weeks. The guide does not say it applies to primary only.
6. "The school has 6 weeks to decide. It can extend this once by up to 4 weeks." Verdict: correct. WPO art 40 lid 6, WEC art 40 lid 7, WVO 8.8 lid 2. Rijksoverheid: "De school heeft 6 weken ... Deze periode kan de school 1 keer met maximaal 4 weken verlengen." Holds today (law text 2026-08-01).
7. "If there is still no decision after 10 weeks and your child is not at another school, your child has a right to a temporary place". Verdict: correct. WPO art 40 lid 7 (10 weeks after the request, child not enrolled elsewhere; temporary placement turns permanent if admitted, ends if refused). Same in WEC art 40 lid 8-9.
8. "the school draws up a development plan (OPP) together with you." Verdict: partly correct. WPO art 40a lid 1: the school sets the OPP "nadat op overeenstemming gericht overleg is gevoerd met de ouders" (consultation aimed at agreement); only the part on individual support (lid 2) needs agreement. Also within 6 weeks of registration (lid 3), evaluated at least once a year (lid 4). Only for pupils who need extra support, and for sbo pupils. Suggested: "...draws up a development plan after talking with you. The part about your child's individual support needs your agreement. This must be done within 6 weeks, and the plan is reviewed with you at least once a year."
9. "Your child has a right to be heard about it." Verdict: correct. WPO art 40a lid 6-8 and WVO 2.44: the school must give the pupil the chance to give an opinion and explain how it influenced the plan. In force 1 Aug 2025 (Wet versterking positie ouders en leerlingen, Stb 2024, 268; art 40a ends with that date in the law history).
10. "Since 1 August 2025 this must be in the school guide (schoolgids). Before that it was a separate document, the school support profile" Verdict: correct. WPO art 13 lid 1 c: guide contains "de basisondersteuningsvoorzieningen en het ondersteuningsaanbod van de school"; art 13b (pupil input) both from 1-8-2025. The search result from Steunpunt Passend Onderwijs (pointer only) confirms the SOP lapsed as a separate document. Holds from 2025-26.
11. "[samenwerkingsverband] arranges extra support, helps find a place when a school cannot give it, and decides on special education." Verdict: correct. WPO art 18a lid 2, 6 (support plan, allocating support, judging eligibility for sbo/so, advising on support needs).
12. "It must also have a support point for parents and young people (ouder- en jeugdsteunpunt)". Verdict: correct. WPO art 19, WVO art 2.47a: "Het samenwerkingsverband zorgt voor een steunpunt ... [dat] ouders en leerlingen informeert ... advies, steun en begeleiding biedt." In force 1 Jan 2025.
13. "SO (speciaal onderwijs) for primary age and VSO for secondary age. There is also a special primary school (sbo)." Verdict: correct. WEC art 40 lid 10/12, WPO art 40 lid 8.
14. "A child can go to sbo, SO or VSO only with a ... TLV. ... that school asks the samenwerkingsverband for the TLV. You do not apply yourself." Verdict: correct. WPO art 40 lid 8 and 18a lid 6c ("op verzoek van het bevoegd gezag van een school ... waar de leerling is aangemeld"), WEC art 40 lid 10-13. Rijksoverheid: "Voordat de school uw kind toelaat, moet de school bij het samenwerkingsverband een toelaatbaarheidsverklaring aanvragen." https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/wetten-en-regelingen/productbeschrijvingen/toelating-leerling-tot-het-speciaal-onderwijs . Precision: the WEC TLV requirement is for SO/VSO cluster 3 and 4.
15. "Experts advise the partnership on whether special education is needed". Verdict: correct. WPO art 18a lid 11. Rijksoverheid: "Deskundigen adviseren het samenwerkingsverband of uw kind (voortgezet) speciaal onderwijs nodig heeft."
16. "Schools for children who are blind or partially sighted, deaf or hard of hearing, or have severe speech and language problems are organised nationally. ... an assessment committee of those schools checks whether a child can be admitted". Verdict: partly verified. Rijksoverheid covers blind, deaf, visually impaired, hard of hearing: "Dan onderzoekt de Commissie voor Onderzoek of uw kind voldoet aan de toelatingseisen." WEC art 40 lid 10 applies the TLV to cluster 3 and 4 only, so clusters 1 and 2 are handled outside the samenwerkingsverband, which fits. "Severe speech and language problems" is not on the cited Rijksoverheid page (not verified here). Suggested: add the committee's name (Commissie voor Onderzoek) and say "ask the school or the cluster 1 and 2 schools".
17. "The samenwerkingsverband decides how long a TLV is valid. For SO and VSO it is at least one full school year." Verdict: correct. WEC art 40 lid 15: TLV "heeft betrekking op een periode van één of meer schooljaren"; if given during the year the time until the next school year is added. WPO art 40 lid 13 uses the same wording for sbo, so "SO and VSO" could say "sbo, SO and VSO".
18. "Practical education in secondary school (praktijkonderwijs) also needs a TLV, and it usually covers the whole time at that school." Verdict: correct on the TLV (WVO art 2.30 lid 2-5: school applies, samenwerkingsverband decides on "toelaatbaarheid"; the school then decides on admission, lid 7). "Usually the whole time": only supported by Steunpunt Passend Onderwijs (not an allowed verdict source; WVO art 2.48 lets a samenwerkingsverband deviate on duration). Treat as not verifiable with an official source; keep "usually".
19. "If special education turns out not to fit, the school looks with you for a better place." Verdict: not verifiable as stated. Law: in the last year of a TLV the school must arrange return or transfer unless continued stay is needed (WEC art 40 lid 15). Not the same thing. Suggested: delete, or "In the last year of a TLV the school arranges a move back to a regular school unless a new TLV is given."
20. Gifted section: "part of the basic offer", "each school decides which programmes", "plusklas", "secondary school decides itself", "begaafdheidsprofielscholen pays extra attention". Verdict: correct. Rijksoverheid: "Onderwijs aan hoogbegaafde kinderen valt binnen het basisaanbod van een school." / "In het voortgezet onderwijs zijn scholen zelf verantwoordelijk voor onderwijs op maat aan hoogbegaafde leerlingen." / "Deze scholen geven extra aandacht aan hoogbegaafde leerlingen." https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/passend-onderwijs/vraag-en-antwoord/hoe-krijgt-mijn-hoogbegaafde-kind-onderwijs . "A primary school describes this in its support offer or school plan" is not on that page (not verified; WPO art 13 lid 1 c does require the support offer in the guide).
21. "You can have your child tested, but no test is required to ask the school what it offers." Verdict: not verifiable (common sense, no official source says otherwise).
22. "A free education adviser (onderwijsconsulent) can help you." Verdict: correct. Rijksoverheid: "kosteloos een beroep doen op een onderwijsconsulent". Same page as claim 1.
23. "TLV decision. You can object (bezwaar maken) to the samenwerkingsverband within six weeks." Verdict: correct. Awb art 6:7 "De termijn ... bedraagt zes weken"; WPO art 40 lid 8 and WEC art 40 lid 14 only refer to art 8:4 lid 3 onder b Awb (a point about court appeal, not about bezwaar); nothing in them removes the bezwaar route, and the samenwerkingsverband must have an advisory committee for these objections (art 18a lid 12). Time starts the day after notification (Awb 6:8).
24. "It then asks an advisory committee, in most regions the national one (Landelijke Bezwaaradviescommissie Toelaatbaarheidsverklaring), before it decides again." Verdict: correct. WPO art 18a lid 12: the samenwerkingsverband must set up an advisory committee (Awb 7:13). Onderwijsgeschillen: "Inmiddels hebben 149 samenwerkingsverbanden zich aangesloten bij de LBT." https://www.onderwijsgeschillen.nl/thema/toelaatbaarheidsverklaring/ (149 is a number as of the page; it will drift).
25. "The development plan. If you cannot agree with the school, you can ask the national disputes committee for passend onderwijs for a ruling." Verdict: partly correct. WPO art 43 lid 2: the committee also handles disputes on admission, the duty of care and removal (art 40 lid 3, 4, 5, 11) as well as the OPP (art 40a lid 1, 2, 5). The page lists it only for the development plan, and the "disputes about admission" bullet only has the TLV objection. Rijksoverheid: "kunt u hierover een oordeel vragen aan de landelijke geschillencommissie passend onderwijs." Also art 43 lid 3: opinion within 10 weeks; the committee is "tijdelijk" in the statute title but exists (Onderwijsgeschillen, GPO). Suggested bullet: "A refusal to admit your child, or a dispute about the duty of care or the development plan. You can ask the national disputes committee for passend onderwijs (Geschillencommissie passend onderwijs) for an opinion, which it gives within 10 weeks." Page says "landelijke geschillencommissie"; the committee's own name is Geschillencommissie passend onderwijs (GPO), https://www.onderwijsgeschillen.nl/commissie/geschillencommissie-passend-onderwijs-gpo/ (listed in a search, not opened).
26. "Ouders & Onderwijs also gives free advice to parents." Verdict: correct. Site shows "Gratis advies!" and 088-6050101 (free). It is an independent foundation funded by the ministry; use as pointer, not verdict.

## Expires / year-bound
- School guide duty (claim 10) and pupil hearing right (claim 9): from 2025-26 onward.
- LBT number 149 (claim 24): as seen 2026-11-25.
- Nothing else is tied to a school year.

## Missing for expat parents
A. Dutch-language support is not "extra support" for the duty of care: WPO art 40 lid 3 and WEC art 40 lid 4: "Onder extra ondersteuning wordt niet verstaan ondersteuning ter bevordering van de beheersing van de Nederlandse taal." So a school cannot use the duty of care to place a newcomer in a newcomer class; the guide only says "a different matter". Worth one sentence.
B. Admission cannot depend on legal residence: WPO art 40 lid 1: "De toelating tot de school is niet afhankelijk van het houden van rechtmatig verblijf in de zin van artikel 8 van de Vreemdelingenwet 2000." Also "De toelating mag niet afhankelijk worden gesteld van een geldelijke bijdrage van de ouders." Relevant to expat and newcomer families; the guide does not say it.
C. Child living abroad: the samenwerkingsverband of the school asked decides on the TLV (WPO art 40 lid 8 b2, WEC art 40 lid 11-13). Useful for families arriving from abroad who apply to special education before moving.
D. For the group 8 to secondary move registration is the central week 25-31 March, not "10 weeks before" (WVO art 8.8 lid 8).
