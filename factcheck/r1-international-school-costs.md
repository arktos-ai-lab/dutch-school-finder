# r1 fact-check: question international-school-costs (checked 2026-11-25)

| # | Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | State-funded Dutch schools charge no tuition | correct (WPO art 40 lid 1; Rijksoverheid) |
| 2 | Private international schools set their own fees | correct |
| 3 | ISA 2026-27 tuition about 22,800 nursery to about 32,800 last two years | correct. isa.nl/fees: Nursery EUR 22,815; Grades 11-12 (DP) EUR 32,755. Holds for 2026-27 |
| 4 | Some schools add a deposit or entry fee | not verifiable. ISA fee page shows none; third party mentions registration and enrolment fees. Safe if kept as "some" |
| 5 | DIS are partly government funded and "usually cost much less" | partly correct. DIS page: "funded by the Ministry"; no official DIS fee data found. Funded departments may require a parental contribution (Kamerstuk 22452 nr 88) |
| 6 | Third-party guides report about 4,500 to 9,500 euro a year | not verifiable. The cited IamExpat page says 4,500 to 9,000 per child per year (as of 2026) and 18,000 to 30,000+ for private schools. Suggested: "about 4,500 to 9,000 euro, from one outside guide" |

Counts: correct 3, partly correct 1, not verifiable 2, wrong 0, outdated 0.

## Missing
- An official funded-school example: International School Eindhoven 2026-27 fee terms list EUR 6,690 a year for the Primary International Department (seen via search only; read the school's own PDF before citing).
- The compulsory parental contribution point (Kamerstuk 22452 nr 88).
