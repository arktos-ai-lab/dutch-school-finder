## Missing for expat parents
- Extra funding: schools get supplementary funding for newcomers in the first 2 years (RO).
- Leerplicht covers every child 5 to 16 living in NL regardless of nationality, so a place must be found even without Dutch (RO leerplicht).
- Kwalificatieplicht to 18 for 16 to 17 year olds without startkwalificatie.
- Shortage of places: the ministry's 5 regional coordinators advise municipalities and school boards, not parents (RO).
