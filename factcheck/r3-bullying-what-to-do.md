# r3 bullying-what-to-do

WPO art 4c read from the XML (version 2026-08-01); WVO 2020 and WEC not read, art 4c WPO is the equivalent named by Inspectie. Checked 2026-11-25.

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Schools have a legal duty to keep pupils socially safe; since August 2015; "School Safety Act (Wet veiligheid op school)" | correct | Inspectie zorgplicht page: "Scholen hebben deze zorgplicht sinds augustus 2015." Law: WPO art 4c(1)-(2), safety means "sociale, psychische en fysieke veiligheid". Staatsblad 2015, 238. |
| Online bullying is part of it | correct | RO aanpak-pesten-school: schools must act on online bullying. |
| Safety policy including bullying; separate pestprotocol allowed, not required | correct | RO: "Een pestprotocol kan een onderdeel van dit plan zijn maar dat is niet verplicht." WPO art 4c(1)(a). |
| A person coordinates anti-bullying work and is the contact point; "at many schools a vertrouwenspersoon" | correct | WPO art 4c(1)(c): tasks "het coördineren van het beleid ... tegengaan van pesten" and "het fungeren als aanspreekpunt". RO: "vaak de schoolvertrouwenspersoon". |
| Yearly check of how safe pupils feel | partly correct | Inspectie: schools monitor pupils' sense of safety "jaarlijks". WPO art 4c(1)(b) only says a monitor giving "een representatief en actueel beeld"; frequency is left to an AMvB (art 4c(3)). Keep "every year" only citing Inspectie. |
| "School must make the results available to the Inspectie" | partly correct | WPO art 4c(4): the board "zendt onverwijld de resultaten van de monitor ... aan de inspectie" (sends them promptly). Inspectie wording is "beschikbaar stelt". Suggest: "The school must send the results to the Inspectie." |
| School guide should say who the contact person is and where the safety policy is | correct | WPO art 13(1)(i) and (n): the guide names the safety policy and the person with the art 4c tasks. |
| Steps: teacher, contact person, school leader, board | partly correct | RO pesten-waar-kan-ik-terecht gives no fixed order: parents, mentor, contact person or vertrouwenspersoon, or school leader. The note says "These steps follow the order the Rijksoverheid gives", which overstates. Suggest: "Rijksoverheid starts with the school (teacher or mentor, contact person, school leader) and then the outside routes." |
| Serious cases: go straight to school leader | not verifiable | RO says the school leader can mediate and refer on; no rule on skipping the teacher. Reasonable advice, not an official rule. |
| Complaints committee: free, most schools in a national committee | correct | RO: "Een klacht indienen kost geen geld"; most schools belong to a landelijke klachtencommissie. |
| "The committee must respond within 4 weeks" | partly correct | RO klacht-indienen pages say "moet binnen 4 weken reageren". WPO art 14(6)-(7): the committee gives its judgement within the time set in the school's regeling; it is the board that must say within 4 weeks of receiving the judgement whether it agrees and what it will do. Suggest: "The school board must say within 4 weeks of the committee's opinion what it will do with it." |
| Committee advice not binding; the board decides | correct | RO: "Het advies van de klachtencommissie is niet bindend." |
| Vertrouwensinspecteurs: serious problems, phone 0900 111 3 111, working days 08:00 to 17:00 | correct | onderwijsinspectie.nl/vertrouwensinspecteurs: that number, "alle werkdagen tijdens kantooruren (08.00-17.00 uur)", examples sexual, psychic, physical violence. Discrimination and severe bullying come via RO. Rate on their page is garbled ("0,028 cent per minuut"), so the page's "small per-minute rate" is safe. |
| Ouders & Onderwijs: free, 088 605 0101, Mon-Fri 09:30-12:30 and 13:00-16:00, WhatsApp | correct | oudersenonderwijs.nl: "088-6050101 (Gratis)", same hours, WhatsApp +31 88 605 0101 (as read 2026-11-25). Not an official government source, so the number can change. |
| Kinderombudsman after earlier complaints; school must say within 4 weeks | correct | RO pesten-waar-kan-ik-terecht: "De school moet binnen 4 weken aangeven wat de school met het advies gaat doen." |
| "The Inspectie does not handle individual complaints, but you can send a copy" | correct | Inspectie: "neemt geen individuele klachten in behandeling". RO: you can send a copy; it may use it in supervision. |
| Serious insults or assault can be a crime; police | not verifiable | RO says the school leader can refer to the police. General criminal law, not read. |

## Missing, important for expat parents
- WPO art 14: parents may complain about discrimination and about acts or decisions of the board or staff; the school's regeling sets the time limit for filing.
- WPO art 8(3a): the board must create a school culture where pupils feel safe and accepted regardless of religion, origin, gender, disability, sexual orientation.
- The school must be able to say in the school guide who the anti-bullying contact is (art 13(1)(n)).
- Language barriers: ask the school for an interpreter or a written summary of meetings. Not in a source read this round, do not state as a right.
