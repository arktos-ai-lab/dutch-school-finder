# r3 choose-a-primary-school

RO = rijksoverheid.nl. WPO = Wet op het primair onderwijs (BWBR0003420, version 2026-08-01, read from the repository.officiele-overheidspublicaties.nl XML). Checked 2026-11-25.

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| "You may register at any primary school that has room, and at more than one" | correct | RO wanneer-kind-aanmelden: "U mag uw kind bij meerdere scholen aanmelden." WPO art 40(2): parents state at registration which other schools were asked. |
| Waiting list, priority "such as brothers and sisters" | partly correct | RO: a school may have no free place or a waiting list. No official page read says siblings get priority. Suggest: "A school may be full or keep a waiting list. Ask which priority rules it uses." |
| Municipalities: neighbourhood-only, lottery, siblings first | partly correct | RO wanneer-mag-mijn-kind: some areas have agreements on how pupils are distributed, check the municipality site. Lottery and siblings-first for primary are not named by RO. Suggest: "Some areas have local agreements on how pupils are distributed. Check your municipality's website." |
| "Register at least 10 weeks before your child turns 4" | correct | RO: "Meld uw kind minimaal 10 weken voordat het 4 jaar wordt aan." WPO art 40(2): "zo mogelijk ten minste 10 weken", so the law says "if possible". |
| "You can register from age 3, not earlier" | correct | WPO art 40(2): "vanaf de dag waarop het kind de leeftijd van 3 jaar bereikt". |
| "Your child may start at 4" | correct | RO: "Uw kind mag vanaf 4 jaar naar de basisschool." |
| "Attendance is compulsory from age 5" | partly correct | RO wanneer-mag-mijn-kind: leerplicht starts on the first day of the month after the child turns 5. Suggest: "Attendance is compulsory from the first day of the month after your child turns 5." |
| Bring BSN and birth certificate or ID; de-registration proof | correct | RO: BSN must be given at registration; "Neem hiervoor het geboortebewijs of identiteitsbewijs van uw kind mee"; proof of deregistration if moving from another school. |
| Older child arriving can register during the school year | correct | WPO art 40 sets no registration window. Not stated on the RO page read; low risk. |
| Public school open to every child, no religion | correct | WPO art 46(2): "toegankelijk voor alle kinderen zonder onderscheid van godsdienst of levensbeschouwing". "If there is room" still applies (art 40(5)(a)). |
| Special school teaches from faith, belief or a teaching concept such as Montessori; concept schools can be public or special | partly correct | RO: bijzondere scholen teach "vanuit een godsdienst, levensovertuiging of een visie op het onderwijs"; concepts can be public or special. "Or a teaching concept" is loose since the types guide treats concept as separate. |
| Religious school "may, in some cases, set conditions", may not discriminate | partly correct | WPO art 40(5)(b): a school may ask parents to declare they respect or subscribe to its basis; refusal removes the duty to find another school for a child needing extra support. Suggest: "A school with a religious basis may ask you to respect that basis, and may ask you to sign a statement. It may not discriminate, for example on origin." |
| Some primary schools run childcare and after-school activities on site | correct | RO: brede scholen offer childcare and after-school activities. WPO art 45(2): the board must arrange childcare if parents ask. |
| Parental contribution voluntary; school may not exclude the child | correct | WPO art 13(1)(e): voluntary, non-payment "niet leidt tot het uitsluiten van leerlingen van deelname aan activiteiten". Art 40(1): admission may not depend on a contribution. |
| Inspectie "mostly gives a judgement after a visit; missing judgement is not a rating" | not verifiable | No source read this round. Belongs to the data section. |

## Missing, important for expat parents
- WPO art 40(1): admission does not depend on lawful residence, and may not depend on a contribution.
- WPO art 40(6): the school decides within 6 weeks of the registration, or says by when (at most 4 more weeks). Art 40(7): after 10 weeks without a decision, a child not at another school is placed temporarily.
- IGBO international departments for families in the Netherlands temporarily (RO basisschool-kiezen page).
- Art 40(3): language support is not "extra ondersteuning", so it cannot be a ground to refuse under the extra-support rules.
