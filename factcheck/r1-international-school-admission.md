## Missing for expat parents
- Legal basis: WPO art 40 lid 10 (admission) and art 85a (funded international departments). Funded departments may only admit pupils who meet these conditions.
- Funded international departments may make the parental contribution compulsory (Kamerstuk 22452 nr 88, 19 Dec 2023: "Bekostigde afdelingen voor internationaal onderwijs mogen de ouderbijdrage verplicht stellen"). The page implies low, fixed fees only.
- Tax: employer-paid school fees for international schools are a separate tax-free item (see r1-thirty-percent-ruling).
