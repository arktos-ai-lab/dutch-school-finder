# r1 bsn-and-address

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Can register without BSN; school enrols with temporary onderwijsnummer issued by DUO | correct | rijksoverheid.nl .../bsn-in-het-onderwijs: "Als iemand (nog) geen bsn heeft, dan wordt gebruikgemaakt van een tijdelijk onderwijsnummer"; DUO creates it. Note the primary page says the BSN must be given at registration; the temporary number is the fallback. |
| Tell the school when child gets a BSN | correct | same page |
| You register your child yourself | correct | passend-onderwijs page: parent registers in writing |
| Live in NL: get BSN by registering with municipality | partly correct / not verifiable | cited pages say the municipality issues the BSN once the child is in the BRP; they do not explain the procedure. Stays under 4 months (RNI) not covered; two government.nl/Rijksoverheid BSN URLs I tried returned 404. |
| Bring passport, proof of address, deregistration or school reports | not verifiable | school registration sources list identity document/birth certificate and deregistration proof. BRP documents are not in the cited sources; cite the municipality site. |
| Amsterdam central procedure can require proof of future address | not verifiable | not in the cited sources; check Amsterdam's own site. |

Gap: the question asks about an address, but no cited source says an address is required to register at a school. Say so only with a source.
