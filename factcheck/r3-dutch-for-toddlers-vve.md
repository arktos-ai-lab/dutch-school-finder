## Source links
- All four load. RO voorschoolse-educatie does not mention costs or childcare benefit. The "wanneer-mag-mijn-kind" URL on this page is /themas/onderwijs/basisonderwijs/vraag-en-antwoord/basisonderwijs/... and loads.
- government.nl childcare-benefit lists only "childcare" and "buitenschoolse opvang". It does not mention peuteropvang or VVE.

## Missing, important for expat parents
- Childcare benefit (kinderopvangtoeslag) can cover peuteropvang as kinderopvang when it is registered in the LRK and the parents work or study (Belastingdienst, RO wanneer-heb-ik-recht). 2026 maximum hourly rate for daycare is EUR 11.23 (RO bedragen 2026); parents pay any excess.
- In many municipalities a VVE place is free or cheap, but this is local and not found in a source read.
- Only a child with an indication qualifies for the VVE place; a child without one can still use ordinary daycare.
