| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Public or special, both state-paid, no tuition | correct | RO openbaar-en-bijzonder-onderwijs: "De overheid betaalt voor beide soorten onderwijs." Conditions: quality, pupil numbers, qualified teachers, teaching hours. |
| Concept is separate from religion; a public school can teach Montessori | correct | RO: Montessori, Dalton, Jenaplan, Vrije scholen can be public or special. |
| Public school open to every child, not based on a religion | correct | WPO art 46(2). |
| Special school: from religion or belief; some have no religion and an educational view | correct | RO: "Bijzondere scholen geven les vanuit een godsdienst, levensovertuiging of een visie op het onderwijs." |
| Voluntary parental contribution possible | correct | WPO art 13(1)(e). |
| DUO groups listed; a few Evangelical, Hindu, Jewish; some combined | correct | Site data (DUO 2025-26, schools_po.json): Evangelisch 12, Hindoestisch 6, Joods 2, combined "Samenwerking" 77, Reformatorisch 170, Gereformeerd vrijgemaakt 112. |
| Catholic and Protestant "more than half of primary school locations" | correct | Same data: RK 1,883 + PC 1,542 = 3,425 of 6,349 (54%) before combined groups. Own data, holds for 2025-26. |
| "For most secondary schools DUO says only bijzonder" | not verifiable | Not checked against schools_vo.json this round. |
| Concept table | partly correct | Montessori (montessori.nl): children "make their own choices, work on their own": ok. Dalton (dalton.nl): core values are cooperation, freedom and responsibility (organise own tasks within limits), reflection, independence; trust appears only in the mission text, so "Built on trust" is thin. Suggest: "Built on freedom with responsibility. Pupils plan their own work within clear limits, work together and reflect." Jenaplan (jenaplan.nl): "leef- en werkgemeenschap met heterogene stamgroepen", gesprek, spel, werk, viering: ok. Vrije school (vrijescholen.nl): anthroposophy, art as important as language and maths, periodeonderwijs of "enkele weken": ok. |
| "A special school may require pupils and teachers to hold its religion or belief" | partly correct | RO: "Een bijzondere school mag van leraren en leerlingen eisen dat ze een godsdienst of levensovertuiging hebben." The law on admission is narrower: WPO art 40(5)(b), the school may ask parents to declare they respect or subscribe to the basis. Suggest: "A special school may ask parents to respect its basis, and may set religious requirements for teachers." |
| "A school may not discriminate ... In a dispute a court decides" | partly correct | RO: special schools may not discriminate. "A court decides" is not on RO. Routes: the school's complaints committee (WPO art 14 names discrimination), the College voor de Rechten van de Mens, then a court. |
| "Home address, waiting lists and local registration rules also decide" | partly correct | RO: waiting lists and municipal agreements. Home address only where a municipality uses a catchment rule. Add "in some areas". |
| Public school must let pupils take GVO/HVO "if parents ask" | partly correct | WPO art 50(1): the board "stelt de leerlingen in de gelegenheid" to take religious or humanist lessons within school hours; at most 40 hours a year count toward the teaching hours; other activities for pupils who do not take part. The school guide must inform parents (art 50(3)). The law has no "if parents ask". Suggest: "A public school must give pupils the chance to take religious or humanist lessons during school hours. The school guide says how." |
| Children who do not take part get other lessons | correct | WPO art 50(1): "andere onderwijsactiviteiten". |
| "At a special school, the school sets its own rules" | partly correct | Only within the law: no discrimination, art 40, art 8(3a) safe school culture. |
| School page shows denomination and concept labels from DUO and association lists | not verifiable | The site's own data, not an official page. |
