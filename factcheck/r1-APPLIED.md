## Guides

**school-holidays-and-leave.** Reworded: autumn and spring dates are advice, a school may choose other dates once its participation council agrees (was "a school can change them"). Confirmed and kept: carnival (the Rijksoverheid 2026-27 page says the south's advisory spring dates miss carnival and schools may align), employer statement and self-employed income loss (leerplicht-schoolvakanties), regions listed per municipality (Rijksoverheid regions page, added as source; a few municipalities are split by village). "Accounts for self-employed" became "proof of the income loss". Added: parents can be punished.

**international-school-admission.** "Partly funds" to "funds" (DIS page: "acknowledged and funded"). Condition 2 now says only Dutch nationality, a parent worked abroad for a set period, school there for more than 2 years, with the DIS page's "at least 2" noted (WPO art 40 lid 10 b). Fee range 4,500 to 9,500 removed (the cited outside guide says 9,000 and is not an allowed source); replaced by official International School Eindhoven 2026-27 fees (6,690 primary; 8,569 to 9,466 secondary; 200 registration fee; its page says its fee is "in line with the other Dutch International Schools"). "That is why they are usually cheaper" replaced (an inference; the law is why there are conditions). Added the compulsory contribution (Kamerstuk 22452 nr 88). Sources added: WPO art 40, ISE fees, Kamerstuk.

**dutch-or-international-school.** "Most families choose a Dutch school" removed (no figure found). "Partly funded" to "funded" in desc, option text and cost. Fee range replaced by the ISE example. "Some schools add deposit or entry fee" to "ask whether a registration fee or deposit comes on top" (ISA lists none; ISE has a registration fee). ISK "many stay about two years" to "the aim is about two years, ask the school" (the Rijksoverheid Q&A is in an asylum context, so hedged). IB sentence hedged ("may offer", IB register; Nuffic names no Dutch schools).

**extra-support-passend-onderwijs.** Duty of care: exceptions (full school, denominational basis), "talk with you and make sure another school is willing", Dutch language support excluded (WPO art 40 lids 3, 4, 5). Step 1: "if possible at least 10 weeks before the date you want your child to start", plus group 8 week 25 to 31 March (WVO 8.8 lid 8). OPP: after talking with you, only the individual-support part needs agreement, 6 weeks, yearly review (art 40a). TLV: required for SO/VSO clusters 3 and 4 only (WEC art 40 lids 10, 12). Cluster 1 and 2 sentence rewritten: the Commissie voor Onderzoek is confirmed for blind, visually impaired, deaf and hard of hearing; "severe speech difficulties" is cluster 2 (WEC art 2 lid 4) but the committee is not confirmed for it, so hedged; "organised nationally" dropped as not confirmed. TLV validity "one or more school years" (WEC art 40 lid 15). Praktijkonderwijs "usually the whole time" removed (no official source; WVO art 2.30 says nothing on duration) and hedged. "School looks with you for a better place" replaced by the art 40 lid 15 last-year rule. Gifted: "describes it in support offer or school plan" to school guide (WPO art 13); "no test is required" removed as unsourced. Disputes committee scope widened (art 43 lid 2; GPO page: admission, removal, OPP; 10 weeks; advisory only). Confirmed unchanged: 6 weeks plus 4, temporary placement after 10 weeks, hearing right and school guide duty from 1 Aug 2025 (Stb 2024, 268), onderwijsconsulent, Ouders & Onderwijs, six-week objection. Dead links replaced: Steunpunt (HTTP 500) by WPO art 40 and 40a and WEC art 40 on wetten.overheid.nl; VO-raad news (HTTP 500) by Stb 2024, 268; added the GPO page.

**school-advice-and-doorstroomtoets.** Double-advice sentence loosened ("can always be registered for the higher level, if the school offers it; a school offering one level need not take the child"); "keeps more options open" to "can keep options open, depending on the school's rules"; "may not place below" now "unless you explicitly ask for a lower level" (2016 letter).

## Question pages (en.json)

**thirty-percent-ruling.** Outdated claim replaced by adopted law (Rijksoverheid news 18 Dec 2024: the Eerste Kamer approved the Belastingplan 2025 on 17 Dec 2024; nota van wijziging 36602 nr 41 sets 27% from 1 Jan 2027). Transitional rules as in the Rijksoverheid table (up to 31-12-2023: 30%, current norm; 2024: 27% in 2027, current norm; from 2025: 27%, new norm). 2025 and 2026: 30%. Cap: calculated on salary up to EUR 262,000 from 2026 (Rijksoverheid; Belastingdienst gives EUR 78,600). School fees: separate item, on top of the 30% (art 31a lid 8, Besluit art 10ea), not touched by the 27% amendment (it replaces "30%" only). "Conditions set by the Belastingdienst" corrected to the statute and decree (Uitvoeringsbesluit loonbelasting 1965, art 10e and 10ea, not "2011"). Without the ruling: tax-free only with a causal link to the temporary stay, none in general if the child attended the school before the posting (KG:041:2023:12). Left out because not confirmed: the new 2027 salary norm figures, the 5-year maximum.

**when-must-child-go.** Leerplicht end: 12 full school years or at the latest the end of the school year in which the child turns 16 (Leerplichtwet art 3 lid 1 a and b; the report said art 4, which covers attendance). Kwalificatieplicht exemptions added (praktijkonderwijs, certain vso tracks, Rijksoverheid). "Every child, whatever nationality" confirmed on Rijksoverheid and kept.

**mid-year-arrival.** "Any time of year" narrowed to secondary when you move house. Documents: identity document or birth certificate and proof of de-registration, school reports only "ask the school". 10-week rule "if possible" and "register as early as you can". Temporary place after 10 weeks added. "Newcomer class can take time" hedged.

**bsn-and-address.** BSN via BRP: more than 4 months, within 5 days, RNI for shorter stays (Rijksoverheid). Document list and Amsterdam proof of future address removed as unconfirmed. Added that we found no national rule that an address is needed to register at a school.

**international-school-costs.** "Partly funded", "usually much less" and the 4,500 to 9,500 range replaced by the ISE example and the compulsory-contribution note. "Some add deposit or entry fee" hedged. IamExpat dropped from this item's sources.

**ib-route.** "Our school list also flags Dutch IB schools" hedged (an IB filter exists; confirm with the school). "At least two years" fixed to more than two, with the DIS wording noted. Report proposal rejected: removing "gives access to higher education". The cited Nuffic secondary-education page says "Function of the diploma: access to higher education" and the level page gives the vwo comparison, so the sentence stays.

**dutch-international-bilingual.** "Often follows the IB" to "may follow"; "partly funded" to "funded"; tto adds vmbo 30 percent and the extra certificate; "small pilot" replaced by a sourced tpo sentence (Nuffic 30 to 50 percent in English, German or French; Rijksoverheid pilot evaluation 2023); "could not confirm a Dutch-German school" dropped as the report proposed.

**secondary-lottery-cities.** Amsterdam rewritten: lot number, placement in lot order, second round, excluded groups (practical education, VSO, tussenvoorziening, Kopklas, ISK), list lengths 12/6/4 for 2027, placement guarantee, children from abroad register with the first-choice school. Rotterdam and Eindhoven hedged (no official page opened). Link: the OCO root replaced by the OCO FAQ page (key `oco`), and schoolkeuze020.nl/de-overstap/ added; both return 200 and state the above.

The half-level in-between rule is confirmed on the already cited Rijksoverheid "advies" page (the timeline page does not carry it). Not applied: the report's praktijkonderwijs exception (outside the owner's list).

**ouderbijdrage-free-school.** "Many ask" to "schools may ask". "Rijksoverheid information line" replaced by MR parent members, Ouders & Onderwijs (088 605 0101) and the Inspectie (088 669 6060).
