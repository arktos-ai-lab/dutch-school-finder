## Praktijkonderwijs to mbo (the unverified edge `std-pro-mbo`)

Claim: arrow pro to mbo, kind "standard", dashed, condition "Conditions, selection or a school decision apply.", sources `mboRijks`, `schema`, checked 2026-11-10 (every other edge is 2026-11-25).

Verdict: PARTLY CORRECT, and the cited source does not support it.
- `mboRijks` (Rijksoverheid "Uit welke mbo-opleidingen kan ik kiezen?") never mentions praktijkonderwijs. It lists vmbo diplomas and "bewijs ... de eerste 3 leerjaren van de havo of het vwo". Nuffic's schema page is an image with no text on pro.
- WEB art 8.1.1b lid 1: "de toelating tot de entreeopleiding [staat] uitsluitend open voor degenen die niet ten minste voldoen aan de vooropleidingseisen van de basisberoepsopleiding ... en op wie paragraaf 2 van de Leerplichtwet 1969 niet meer van toepassing is". Lid 2: the college may refuse only if the person was enrolled in an entree programme in the past two study years. So a pro leaver (no vmbo diploma) has a right to mbo level 1 once full-time compulsory schooling has ended. That is not "selection or a school decision".
- WEB art 8.2.1 lid 4: level 2 requires a vmbo/mavo diploma, proof of havo/vwo year 3, another designated diploma, or "f. een diploma entreeopleiding". Lid 6: the college may exempt in special cases after an aptitude test. So a pro leaver reaches level 2 only through the entree diploma (or an exemption). Neither the pro school certificate (WVO art 2.58/2.59) nor a pro diploma is a listed entry document.
- WVO art 2.8 lid 2: praktijkonderwijs "is ingericht om voor te bereiden op functies binnen de regionale arbeidsmarkt op een niveau onder dat van de entreeopleiding". Mbo is not the statutory aim of pro. The Kamer study "Doorstroom van praktijkonderwijs naar mbo" (KBA Nijmegen/NRO 2018, https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/blg-834740.pdf) says: "In feite geeft alleen het entree-diploma behaald in het praktijkonderwijs recht op doorstroom (naar niveau 2)" and that about 40 percent of pro leavers go on to mbo. That figure is 2018, not current.
- Suggested edge (kind "right" to level 1, "conditional" to level 2): "A practical education leaver can start the mbo entry programme (level 1) once full-time compulsory schooling has ended, without a vmbo diploma. Level 2 needs the entry programme diploma or a diploma such as vmbo, unless the college exempts you after an aptitude test." Sources to add: WEB art 8.1.1b, 8.2.1 lid 4 and 6; KiesMBO entreeopleiding page (https://www.kiesmbo.nl/over-het-mbo/entreeopleiding: "Met dit diploma kun je doorstromen naar een mbo-opleiding op niveau 2"; it does not mention pro). Replace `mboRijks` for this edge.
- Dashed line on the diagram is acceptable for "not automatic", but the card text above should replace the generic condition string. Date 2026-11-10 must be refreshed.

## Missing for expat parents (with source)
- A child arriving from abroad has no Dutch school advice. WVO art 8.6 lid 5 a: an AMvB regulates "de beslissing tot toelating tot het eerste leerjaar indien geen definitief schooladvies is vastgesteld", and lid 4 lets the Inspectie allow deviation. The page and diagram say nothing about this case.
- mbo application deadline: apply by 1 April, later gives no right to admission (WEB art 8.1.1c lid 3 c; Rijksoverheid page "wat-zijn-de-toelatingseisen-voor-het-mbo"). Appears on the home route page but not in the system cards that call the routes "a right".
- Right to mbo can be limited for capacity or labour market prospects (WEB art 8.1.1c lid 2).
- Entry programme (level 1) is the route for anyone without a diploma, including arrivals with foreign schooling.
