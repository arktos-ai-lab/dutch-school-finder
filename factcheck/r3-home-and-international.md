Scope: `hero.*`, `home.*`, `stops.*`, `path.*`, `next.*`, `adv.dates`, `age.*`, `intl.*`. School advice wording is in r1-school-advice-how-it-works.md and not repeated. ## Missing for expat parents (with source)
- Home steps do not say that children who arrive from abroad have no Dutch advice and that secondary admission then follows WVO 2020 art 8.6 lid 5 a (AMvB rules for admission without a final advice).
- Home steps do not mention the doorstroomtoets exemption for children four years or less in the Netherlands (Rijksoverheid, wanneer-geen-doorstroomtoets-maken).
- mbo level 1 route for children without a diploma (practical education leavers, late arrivals) is on the page but the right to start it (WEB art 8.1.1b) is not stated.
