# r1 fact-check: question dutch-international-bilingual (checked 2026-11-25)

| # | Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dutch school teaches in Dutch, Dutch curriculum, state pays, no tuition | correct (WPO art 40 lid 1; Rijksoverheid) |
| 2 | International school teaches in English or another language and "often follows a programme such as the IB" | not verifiable. No official source on how common the IB is; Rijksoverheid only says other international schools are private |
| 3 | DIS are "partly government funded", for internationally mobile families | partly correct. DIS page: "funded by the Ministry"; "partly" not stated |
| 4 | Fully private international schools set their own fees | correct |
| 5 | tto: Dutch secondary school, at least half of the lessons in the first years of havo and vwo in English | correct. Rijksoverheid: "In de onderbouw havo/vwo is minstens 50% van de lessen in de andere taal"; Nuffic: "minimaal de helft van de vakken in het Engels". Omits vmbo at 30% |
| 6 | Pupils take the normal Dutch exams | correct ("doen in het Nederlands eindexamen") |
| 7 | "Bilingual primary schools exist as a small pilot" | not verifiable. Nuffic describes primary tpo as 30 to 50 percent of teaching time in English, German or French but the page read did not call it a pilot or give its size. Check the Nuffic tpo page |

Matches Nuffic, but Rijksoverheid words tto neutrally as "de andere taal". Suggested: "Nuffic describes English for secondary tto and English, German or French for primary" and drop the unconfirmed-school remark |
