## Source links

- RO timeline, inschrijven, toelating gebaseerd op definitief advies, tto: all load. The 2027 timeline supports every date in the table. The inschrijven page does not mention the 6-week rule; WVO art 8.8(2) does.

## Missing, important for expat parents

- Pupils from abroad have no Dutch advice: UB art 8.3(1); WVO art 8.6(4) lets the inspectie allow deviation where the advice rule cannot be applied.
- Admission to year 1 cannot be conditional (WVO art 8.6(3)).
- A school may refuse when full, or when parents refuse to respect the religious or philosophical basis (WVO art 8.9(3)).
- Admission may not depend on a payment other than one set by law (WVO art 8.8(7)).
- For a bijzondere school a refusal must be written with reasons, and parents can object within 6 weeks (art 8.8(4), (5)).
