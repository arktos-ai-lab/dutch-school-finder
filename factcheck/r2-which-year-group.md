# r2 which-year-group (guide, checked 2026-11-22)

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Child may start at 4 | correct | RO wanneer-mag-mijn-kind-naar-de-basisschool: "vanaf 4 jaar". |
| Leerplicht begins first day of month after 5th birthday | correct | RO leerplicht: "De leerplicht begint op de 1e dag van de nieuwe maand nadat het kind 5 is geworden." The table's "so in group 1 or 2" is inference, not on RO. Suggest "usually in group 2". |
| Compulsory for every child living in NL | correct | RO leerplicht: children aged 5 to 16 living in NL, regardless of nationality or asylum status. |
| No national rule for the group; the school decides | partly verified | RO blijven-zitten: "De school beslist of uw kind overgaat naar de volgende groep of klas." "Hier bestaan geen wettelijke regels voor." That is about moving up and repeating; for placement of arrivals RO has no rule (inferred, not stated). |
| 1 October rule ended in August 1985 | correct | RO: "Deze regeling bestaat sinds augustus 1985 niet meer." Old rule: age 6 before 1 October. RO says some schools still apply it. "Most schools look at development" is not on RO. Suggest: "Some schools still use the old date. Many look at development; the school guide explains the policy." |
| Age per group table; eight groups, group 1 about age 4 to group 8 about 11 to 12 | not verifiable (official table) | No age-per-group table on the fetched RO pages. Page labels it "our own summary". Acceptable. |

| Kleuterverlenging | missing | RO blijven-zitten does not mention the term; only the group 2 to 3 transition, assessed by the school and discussed with parents. Suggest naming the term (an extra year in the kleuter groups) with "no legal rules, school decides". |
| Parents must be told if a child repeats; objection via school complaints procedure | correct | RO: parents must be informed; "bezwaar maken via de klachtenprocedure van de school." |

## Sources
- RO wanneer-mag: 200; supports start at 4, leerplicht, multiple registrations. Also says registration is possible at 3 and up to 5 wendagen from 3 years 10 months (not all schools), which the guide misses.
- RO blijven-zitten: 200; supports who decides, no legal rules, 1985, objection.
- RO leerplicht: 200; supports compulsory rule.

## Missing for expat parents
- Registration from age 3 and wendagen (RO wanneer-mag).
- Parents can be prosecuted once leerplicht applies; ends after 12 full school years or at end of school year of turning 16; kwalificatieplicht 16 to 18 (RO leerplicht).
