# r1: question secondary-lottery-cities (checked 2026-11-25)

Text under check (en.json / _site/questions/secondary-lottery-cities): lottery where a school has more applicants than places; Amsterdam one central round, random lot number, ranked list, mandatory; Utrecht regions lottery at schools with more first-choice applications than places, 2026 twelve schools; Haaglanden eight schools drew lots in 2026; Rotterdam and Eindhoven own procedures; Haarlem and Nijmegen not confirmed.

Law behind all of it: WVO 2020 art 8.6 lid 2 (admission on the final advice), 8.8 lid 8 (registration 25-31 March). Inspectie, "Feiten en fabels" (Oct 2024): "Wel mag een VO-school een plaatsingsbeleid hanteren waarbij broertjes of zusjes voorrang krijgen of waarbij geloot wordt." So lotteries and sibling priority are allowed by the Inspectie.

## Claims

1. "Where a school has more applicants than places, a lottery can be used." Verdict: correct (Inspectie quote above). Not year-bound.
2. Amsterdam: "places all pupils in one central round with a random lot number and a ranked list of schools." Verdict: partly correct. Official regional pages (schoolkeuze020.nl, run with the municipality and the Amsterdam school boards; OCO Amsterdam for pointers): "Elke leerling krijgt een lotnummer toegewezen door de computer"; the computer places pupils in lot-number order at the highest school on their list with room. But (a) not all pupils: pupils for praktijkonderwijs, VSO, tussenvoorziening (kovo), Kopklas and ISK do not take part; (b) there are two rounds: main round 25-31 March 2027 (results 15 April 2027), second round 15-19 April 2027 (results 22 April 2027, no placement guarantee); (c) late applicants miss the first round. Sources: https://www.schoolkeuze020.nl/de-overstap/ , https://www.schoolkeuze020.nl/veelgestelde-vragen/ , https://ocoamsterdam.nl/vragen/veelgestelde-vragen-over-de-centrale-loting-matching . Holds for entry 2027-28 (page says "Taking part": dates given are 2027).
   Suggested: "Amsterdam places pupils for regular secondary schools in one central round (second round for late applicants). A computer gives each pupil a random lot number and places pupils, in lot order, at the highest school on their ranked list that still has room. Pupils for practical education, special secondary education, Kopklas and ISK register another way."
3. "Taking part is mandatory there." Verdict: not verifiable as worded. schoolkeuze020.nl: "Voordat je naar de middelbare school kunt, moeten je ouder(s)/verzorger(s) je eerst aanmelden", and registration runs online through the parent portal; but none of schoolkeuze020.nl, OCO Amsterdam or the Schoolwijzer Amsterdam FAQ uses the word "verplicht" for the lottery, and the page does not show what happens to someone who applies to a school directly. The Amsterdam gemeente pages gave 403 to automated fetch. Suggested replacement: "To get a place at a regular Amsterdam secondary school you register online through the central procedure in the registration week." Do not write "mandatory" unless the owner finds an official sentence (ask the Adviespunt OCO 020 330 63 20 or schoolkeuze020 servicedesk).
4. Amsterdam list length (not on the page, but a key practical fact). Official: 12 schools for havo, havo/vwo, vwo; 6 for vmbo-gl-tl and vmbo-gl-tl/havo; 4 for vmbo-b, vmbo-k. A full list is what gives the placement guarantee (OCO). Good addition for a page that mentions "a ranked list of schools". Holds for 2027.
5. Utrecht: "In the Utrecht regions a lottery is held at schools that have more first-choice applications than places." Verdict: correct for the first step. Samen naar het VO (the regional body): "Dit jaar vond op twaalf scholen een loting plaats onder leerlingen die deze school als eerste voorkeur hadden opgegeven." The lotting follows the Boston mechanism (first preference, then second preference rounds for pupils still unplaced), so pupils can also be drawn at a lower preference round; each region keeps its own rules and priority rules (Samen naar het VO procedure PDFs 2026-27, found via search, not opened in full). https://www.samennaarhetvo.nl/aktueel/persbericht-2026/
6. "In 2026 this happened at twelve schools." Verdict: correct, but narrower than "the Utrecht regions": the press release covers four regions: Utrecht & Stichtse Vecht, Utrecht West, Zuid-Utrecht, Zuidoost Utrecht. It is intake 2026-27 (not 2027-28). Also useful: 95% of 9,329 pupils placed at first choice, 99% in top three (same release).
7. "In Haaglanden eight schools drew lots in 2026." Verdict: correct. BOVO Haaglanden: eight schools named (Avicenna Lyceum, Christelijk College de Populier, Dalton Den Haag, Edith Stein College, Maerlant Lyceum, Maris College Kijkduin, 's Gravendreef College, Segbroek College); registration 18-31 March; pupils not drawn go into a central lottery for a place at another school on their list. https://www.bovohaaglanden.nl/uitslag-aanmeldingen-middelbare-school-haaglanden-2026/ (opened with the fetch tool; curl got 403). The page says Haaglanden not The Hague: The Hague falls inside it, good. Intake 2026-27; dates for 2027 not checked.
   Missing for this page: the second step (central lottery after school lotteries) described by BOVO.
8. "Rotterdam and Eindhoven have their own procedures." Verdict: correct in substance, not verified from the municipality. Eindhoven: regional folder RSV PVO 2026-27 (a.storyblok.com/f/100722/x/0f32b7e3ed/folder-aanmelden-po-vo-26-27-web-rsv-pvo.pdf, found via search, not opened): central registration week 25-31 March 2027, Boston algorithm. Rotterdam: only school-level information found (Wolfert: lottery under a notary when a brugklas is oversubscribed, schools draw on the same day). No official rotterdam.nl page found. Suggested: keep the sentence, or name "region RSV PVO" for Eindhoven, and link the regional folder.
9. "We could not confirm the rules for Haarlem or Nijmegen." Verdict: honest, still not verifiable from official sources. Haarlem: only press (NH Nieuws) about a rule of 7 preferences and one region at a time; no gemeente source found. Nijmegen: a regional PO-VO protocol (BEVO) exists; Canisius College states a lottery above 220 registrations for 2026-27; no municipal page found. Keep as is.
10. "Procedures can change every year, so read the current guide of your region and ask your child's primary school." Verdict: correct. Expiry statement is good.
11. Related national facts that the page could carry, from the Rijksoverheid timeline 2027: central registration week 25-31 March 2027, final advice by 24 March, decision within six weeks and no later than 12 May 2027 (9 June if late and communicated). WVO 8.8 lid 8, 2 and Rijksoverheid https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/schooladvies-en-doorstroomtoets-basisschool/tijdlijn-schooladvies-2027 . Also schools may not make admission conditional (WVO 8.6 lid 3).

## Expires / year-bound
- Twelve schools (Utrecht), eight schools (Haaglanden): intake 2026-27 only.
- Amsterdam dates (25-31 March 2027, 15 April, 15-19 April, 22 April): intake 2027-28. The page itself gives no dates.

## Missing for expat parents
- Amsterdam: the 12/6/4 list length by advice (the number of schools you must rank), and that the advice is binding on which school types you may rank (schoolkeuze020: "Het basisschooladvies is bindend").
- Pupils arriving from abroad without a Dutch advice, or after the central window, miss the first Amsterdam round (second round only); the page says nothing about it.
- A child from abroad without a school advice: see guide school-advice-and-doorstroomtoets (admission then rests on the school's assessment; WVO 8.6 lid 5 AMvB).
