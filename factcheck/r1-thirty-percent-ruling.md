# r1 fact-check: question thirty-percent-ruling (checked 2026-11-25)

Sources: Wet LB 1964 art 31a (repository XML, version 2026-10-08), Uitvoeringsbesluit loonbelasting 1965 art 10e, 10ea (version 2024-01-01 only; no later consolidation in the repository), Belastingdienst pages "Inhoud van de expatregeling" and "Extraterritoriale kosten", Belastingdienst kennisgroep KG:041:2023:12, Rijksoverheid page on the expat ruling, Ondernemersplein/business.gov.nl, Kamerstuk 36602 nr 41.

Note on names: the owner's brief says "Uitvoeringsbesluit LB 2011". The decree that holds the school-fee and ruling rules is the Uitvoeringsbesluit loonbelasting 1965 (BWBR0002489). The 2011 instrument is the Uitvoeringsregeling loonbelasting 2011 (a ministerial regulation).

## What applies, 2026 and 2027
- 2026: ruling holders can have up to 30% of the wage base tax-free (Wet LB art 31a lid 8; Besluit art 10ea). The 30/20/10 phase-down (art 10ea lid 1 onder a, as in the 2024-01-01 text) was reversed: the Kamerstuk 36602 nr 41 proposal and Rijksoverheid give a constant 30% for 2025 and 2026. I could not retrieve a post-2024 consolidation of art 10ea, so the repeal itself is confirmed only through Rijksoverheid and the Kamerstuk, not the article text.
- 2027: maximum 27% (Ondernemersplein: "Deze wet is aangenomen", in force 1 January 2027). Rijksoverheid table: ruling started up to 31-12-2023 keeps 30% and the current salary norm; started in 2024 gets 27% from 2027 with the current norm; started from 1-1-2025 gets 27% with the new norm. Salary norm 2026 is EUR 48,013 (Rijksoverheid); 2024 base values EUR 46,107 and EUR 35,048 (under 30 with master) rise to EUR 50,436 and EUR 38,388 in 2027 prices (Ondernemersplein). The wage base is capped at EUR 262,000 from 2026.
- School fees: separate from the 30%. Wet LB art 31a lid 8: the ruling counts "tot ten hoogste 30% ... alsmede tot het bedrag van de ... schoolgelden"; Besluit art 10ea lid 1: 30% of the base "b. het bedrag van de schoolgelden". Definition, Besluit art 10e lid 8: "uitgaven voor het door kinderen van de extraterritoriale werknemer volgen van basisonderwijs of voortgezet onderwijs aan internationale scholen en internationale afdelingen van niet-internationale scholen, tot de bedragen die door de school overeenkomstig haar tarieven ... in rekening worden gebracht, met uitzondering van kosten van kost en inwoning maar met inbegrip van vervoerskosten". Belastingdienst: "Het vergoeden van deze schoolgelden staat dus los van de expatregeling." I found no source that the 2027 change touches the school-fee rule.

## Suggested replacement for the second paragraph
"From 1 January 2027 the tax-free allowance under the 30% ruling is 27% for most people. If your ruling started before 2024 you keep 30% for the full term; if it started in 2024 you move to 27% in 2027. School fees for an international school are a separate tax-free item and are not part of the 30% or 27%. Do not choose a school only because of a tax rule. Ask your employer's HR or a tax adviser, and read the current Belastingdienst pages."

## Missing for expat parents
- Reimbursement of school fees can also be tax-free without the ruling as actual extraterritorial costs, but the employer must show a causal link with the temporary stay; there is in principle none if the child already attended that school before the posting (Belastingdienst KG:041:2023:12).
- Fees are covered up to the school's own tariff; board and lodging excluded, transport included (Besluit art 10e lid 8).
- Maximum duration 5 years (Wet LB art 31a lid 8).
