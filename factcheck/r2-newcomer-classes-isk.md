# r2 newcomer-classes-isk (guide, checked 2026-11-22)

RO newcomers = https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/basisonderwijs/onderwijs-voor-nieuwkomers (fetched in Dutch 2026-11-25). Law XML not fetched; claims settled from Rijksoverheid.

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Primary: taalklas or schakelklas; or regular class plus extra Dutch | correct | RO: "via taalklassen of schakelklassen extra taalonderwijs krijgen"; a child may also go straight to a regular class with extra lessons. |
| ISK from age 12, recent arrivals, little or no Dutch | correct | RO: "internationale schakelklassen (isk's) voor jongeren vanaf 12 jaar, die sinds kort in Nederland zijn en nog geen of weinig Nederlands spreken." |
| ISK teaches Dutch plus maths and citizenship, prepares for vo or mbo | correct | RO: besides Dutch, subjects such as wiskunde (rekenen) and Burgerschap; prepares for regular vo or mbo. |
| No tuition fee; voluntary ouderbijdrage may be asked | correct (RO), "voluntary" not on this page | RO: primary and secondary education are free; "Voor extra activiteiten moeten ouders soms wel een bijdrage betalen." See r1-ouderbijdrage-free-school. |
| Lede and how-long: "pupils spend on average about two years in the ISK" (VO-raad) | not verifiable (official) | RO gives no fixed ISK duration. VO-raad page returned HTTP 500 at fetch; a search snippet says VO-raad cites two years on average. Sector council = pointer only. Kamervragen 2023 (2023Z05589) asked for the real average; no official figure found. Suggest: "The VO-raad, the council of secondary schools, says pupils spend about two years in the ISK on average. There is no fixed or official national length." |
| "Schools get extra funding for a newcomer for the first two years" | correct | RO: "Scholen ontvangen voor nieuwkomers in de eerste 2 jaar aanvullende bekostiging." Paid 4 times a year. |
| "In an emergency a municipality can open a temporary newcomer facility; a pupil may be enrolled there for two years at most" | partly wrong | RO: the school board starts a tijdelijke nieuwkomersvoorziening, only with the minister's permission. The municipality (college van B&W) can ask the minister, and only in an emergency. Only pupils not previously enrolled in a Dutch school; "niet langer dan 2 jaar ingeschreven" in total. Not allowed for praktijkonderwijs or special education. Suggest: "In an emergency a municipality can ask the education minister for permission for a temporary newcomer facility, which a school board runs. A pupil can be enrolled there for two years at most." |
| Municipalities and school boards can ask the ministry's regional coordinators for help | correct | RO: 5 regiocoordinatoren nieuwkomersonderwijs "ondersteunen, informeren en adviseren schoolbesturen en gemeenten". Municipalities and boards must consult at least once a year (since October 2023). |
| "No single national upper age for the ISK" | not verifiable | RO gives only "vanaf 12 jaar". Not contradicted. |
| "In primary school parents can usually choose" | not verifiable | RO lists both options, does not say who decides. Suggest: "Both options exist; ask the school what it offers." |
| Contact municipality or school for a place | correct | RO: "De gemeente, opvanglocatie of dichtstbijzijnde school kan informatie geven." Add opvanglocatie (reception site). |
| Classes can be full, waiting lists | not verifiable (official) | RO mentions shortages of places generally. Fine as hedge. |
| Level after ISK decided by receiving school | not verifiable | RO says only that the ISK prepares for vo or mbo. |

## Missing for expat parents
- Leerplicht covers every child aged 5 to 16 living in NL, regardless of nationality or asylum status (RO leerplicht).
- Kwalificatieplicht 16 to 18 without startkwalificatie (havo, vwo, mbo 2), relevant to 16+ newcomers (RO).
- Parents can ask the opvanglocatie (if in one) for school information (RO).
