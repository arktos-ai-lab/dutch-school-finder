## Kinderopvangtoeslag 2026 (holds for calendar year 2026; page states no figures)
- Maximum hourly rate: daycare EUR 11.23, BSO EUR 9.98, gastouder EUR 8.49 (RO bedragen-kinderopvangtoeslag-2026; Belastingdienst calculation document matches).
- Maximum 230 hours per child per month.
- Maximum rate 96% of the allowed amount for lower incomes (up to about EUR 56,000 combined), falling with income. The hourly maximum is not a price; parents pay any excess.
- 2027 rates published September 2026: EUR 11.60, EUR 10.31, EUR 8.77 (not for this page).
- Benefit for BSO runs from age 4 until the first day of the month the child starts secondary school (Belastingdienst).
