## Missing, important for expat parents

- Language is not a legal ground to repeat, and Dutch language support is not "extra ondersteuning" under WVO art 8.9(1).
- No legal limit on repeating found in WVO 2020 or the UB. Only praktijkonderwijs has an age limit (WVO art 8.7: not after the school year in which the pupil turns 18, inspectie may allow an extra year). Bekostigingsbesluit not read, so funding limits are not verified.
- A pupil who repeats past 16: leerplicht ends at the end of the school year in which the child turns 16 (Lpw art 3), then kwalificatieplicht runs to 18 or a startkwalificatie (art 4a, 4b), so the pupil must stay enrolled.
