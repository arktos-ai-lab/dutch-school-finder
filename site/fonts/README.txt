Self-hosted web fonts (woff2, latin subset). Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1;
the licence texts and copyright lines are in the OFL-*.txt files in this folder.

- Schibsted Grotesk (variable, weight 400 to 900): Schibsted Grotesk Project Authors, https://github.com/schibsted/schibsted-grotesk
- Atkinson Hyperlegible (400, 700, 400 italic): Braille Institute of America, Inc., https://github.com/googlefonts/atkinson-hyperlegible

The woff2 files are the unmodified latin subsets from the Fontsource packages (@fontsource-variable/schibsted-grotesk 5.3.0
and @fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible 5.3.0).
